Trading Rules

Please read our trading rules carefully before starting your challenge.

Profit Target

1 Step: 10% profit target. 2 Step: 8% (Phase 1) + 5% (Phase 2). Must reach target to pass evaluation.

Daily Loss Limit

Maximum 3% daily loss based on initial balance. If exceeded at any point during the day, account is breached.

Max Overall Loss

Maximum 6% overall loss from initial balance. Account is terminated if this limit is exceeded at any time.

Minimum Trading Days

1 Step: Minimum 3 trading days. 2 Step: Minimum 5 trading days per phase. At least one trade must be placed.

News Trading

News trading is fully allowed. Trade before, during, and after high-impact news events without any restrictions.

Overnight Holding

Overnight holding is allowed. Keep positions open over the weekend or news events as per your strategy.

Unlimited Retakes

Failed your challenge? No worries — get free retakes on your 1-step plan. We believe in giving second chances.

Prohibited Strategies

Martingale, grid trading without SL, latency arbitrage, tick scalping, and HFT exploiting feed errors are prohibited.
